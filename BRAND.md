# ExpedienteCheck — Brand Manual v1

## Brand Positioning

ExpedienteCheck helps monitor, prevent, and understand risks in public infrastructure projects through intelligent tracking, operational clarity, and applied technology.

### Core Narrative
> Technology does not replace technical judgment.  
> It strengthens it.

# Reel Structure

## Hook
Start with:
- data
- alerts
- questions
- risks
- operational problems

Examples:
- “12 projects required immediate attention.”
- “Why are technical files still being rejected?”
- “S/ 8M remains unexecuted.”
- “70% of delays begin with poor tracking.”

---

## Development
Explain simply:
- what happened
- why it matters
- how to prevent it

No excessive jargon.

---

## Closing
End with:
- operational insight
- preventive thinking
- subtle CTA

Examples:
- “Better tracking prevents delays.”
- “AI helps detect risks earlier.”
- “What variable are you monitoring?”
